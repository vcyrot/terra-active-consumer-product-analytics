# Terra Active — Consumer & Product Analytics Platform

Terra Active is a fictional premium European activewear brand used to build an end-to-end consumer and product analytics portfolio project.

The project explores customer behaviour, product performance, digital conversion, community engagement, marketing effectiveness, and weather-driven inventory decisions.

## Project Goals

- Analyse customer retention and lifetime value
- Identify product and cross-selling opportunities
- Analyse digital purchase funnels
- Measure the impact of community events
- Compare customer acquisition channels
- Improve weather-informed demand and inventory planning
