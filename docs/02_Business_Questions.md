# Terra Active — Business Questions

Business questions are classified as:

- **P1:** Core question to be addressed in the final portfolio
- **P2:** Supporting analysis
- **P3:** Potential future extension


## 1. Customer & Retention

### P1

- What distinguishes high-value repeat customers from one-time customers?
- Which customer segments generate the highest customer lifetime value?
- Which customer behaviours are associated with stronger retention?

### P2

- How long does it typically take customers to make their second purchase?
- Do Urban Runners purchase more frequently than other customer segments?
- Which first-purchase categories are associated with stronger subsequent retention?

## 2. Product & Merchandising

### P1

- Which products and categories drive revenue versus profitability?
- Where are the strongest accessory cross-selling opportunities?
- What factors are associated with higher product return rates?

### P2

- How do new collections perform during their first 30, 60 and 90 days?
- Which colours and product attributes perform best across customer segments?
- How does discounting affect volume, margin and subsequent purchasing behaviour?

## 3. Digital Product

### P1

- Where are the largest points of friction in the digital purchase funnel?
- Which digital behaviours and app features are associated with conversion and retention?

### P2

- How does digital behaviour differ between purchasers and non-purchasers?
- Do personalised recommendations improve conversion or basket size?
- Is challenge participation associated with subsequent app engagement?

## 4. Community & Events

### P1

- Is event participation associated with higher subsequent retention and spending?
- Which event types and European locations offer the strongest opportunities for Terra Active?

### P2

- What factors are associated with event attendance after registration?
- What products are purchased before and after different event types?
- Do customers participating in multiple event types show stronger engagement?

## 5. Marketing & Acquisition

### P1

- Which acquisition channels generate the highest-value customers?

### P2

- Which campaigns generate the strongest commercial performance?
- Which acquisition channels work best for different customer segments?
- Which campaigns are most effective at driving Club membership or event registration?
- Which customers demonstrate the greatest discount dependency?

## 6. Inventory, Weather & Seasonality

### P1

- How does weather affect demand across product categories?
- Can weather information improve short-term inventory planning?
- What is the commercial impact of product stockouts?

### P2

- How does weather affect event registration, cancellation and attendance?
- Which products exhibit the strongest seasonality?
- Where does Terra Active systematically experience overstock or understock?
