# Terra Active — Project Definition

## 1. Company Overview

Terra Active is a fictional premium European activewear brand combining
technical performance, minimalist design and an active outdoor lifestyle.

The company sells performance apparel and accessories through its e-commerce
platform and mobile application.

Terra Active targets consumers who use technical apparel both for sport and
as part of their everyday wardrobe.

## 2. Mission

Enable people to embrace an active lifestyle through thoughtfully designed,
high-performance products inspired by nature.

**Brand philosophy:** Move with Nature.

## 3. Brand Positioning

Terra Active operates in the premium activewear segment.

Its positioning combines:
- Technical performance
- Minimalist design
- Outdoor lifestyle
- Sustainability
- Community
- Digital customer experience

## 4. Geographic Scope

Terra Active operates across major European markets, including:
- United Kingdom
- France
- Germany
- Italy
- Spain
- Switzerland
- Netherlands

## 6. Customer Segments

### Urban Runner
Runs regularly and integrates technical activewear into their everyday
wardrobe. Expected to purchase relatively frequently and engage strongly
with new collections and community running events.

### Outdoor Explorer
Interested in hiking and trail activities. Purchases less frequently but
typically buys higher-value technical products.

### Fitness Enthusiast
Participates in gym, Pilates and related activities. Strong interest in
apparel, new collections and community wellness events.

### Everyday Active
Exercises recreationally and has lower purchase frequency and greater
price sensitivity.

### Performance Athlete
Highly engaged with sport and technical product attributes. Likely to
participate in events and Terra Active Club.

## 7. Terra Active Club

Terra Active Club is the company's community and loyalty programme.

Members can:
- Earn points
- Attend community events
- Complete challenges
- Receive early access to product launches
- Receive personalised recommendations
- Earn rewards for reviews and referrals

## 8. Community & Events

Terra Active organises events across Europe including:
- Running
- Trail running
- Hiking
- Pilates
- Yoga / mobility
- Seasonal challenges

## 9. Digital Ecosystem

The Terra Active mobile app allows customers to:
- Browse products
- Purchase products
- Register for events
- Join challenges
- Manage Club membership
- Save favourite products
- Receive recommendations
- Leave reviews

## 10. Business Objectives

### Growth
- Acquire more high-value customers
- Increase customer retention
- Increase customer lifetime value

### Commercial
- Increase average order value
- Increase accessory attachment rate
- Improve product conversion
- Reduce returns

### Community
- Increase Club membership
- Increase event participation
- Strengthen relationships between community engagement and brand loyalty

### Operations
- Improve inventory planning
- Reduce stockouts
- Incorporate weather and seasonality into demand planning

## 11. Analytics Project Objective

The objective of this portfolio project is to design an end-to-end
consumer and product analytics environment for Terra Active.

The project will combine synthetic relational data, public datasets and
external APIs to analyse customer behaviour, product performance, digital
engagement, community participation, marketing effectiveness and
weather-driven demand.
